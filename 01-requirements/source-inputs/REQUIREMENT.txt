
🚀 AI-Powered Recruitment & Job Portal Platform (Master Product Requirement Document)
1. Product Vision
Build a scalable recruitment platform similar to leading job portals that combines traditional recruitment workflows
with AI-powered candidate matching, intelligent resume parsing, automated screening, interview scheduling, referrals,
  analytics, and real-time collaboration.
The platform must support multiple user roles, high performance, secure authentication, background document processing,
intelligent search, and future scalability without over-engineering the initial version.

we want to design an app which can handle the upload of 1000 resume at the same time and does not crash. 
also aiming to keep the cost minimum for client.

2. User Roles
Candidate (Job Seeker)
The candidate should be able to:
	• Register
	• Login
	• Upload resume/CV
	• Browse jobs
	• Search jobs
	• Apply with account
	• Apply without account (Guest Apply)
	• Track applications
	• Receive interview invitations
	• Schedule interviews
	• Receive AI-generated screening questions
	• View profile match score
	• View missing skills/gap analysis
	• Submit feedback
	• Refer other candidates

Employer
Employer should be able to:
	• Create company profile
	• Manage company information
	• Create multiple HR users
	• Manage HR permissions
	• Create jobs
	• Manage jobs
	• View applicants
	• Review shortlisted candidates
	• Manage interview pipeline
	• Receive live notifications
	• View recruitment analytics

HR
HR works under Employer.
Permissions are configurable.
Depending on permissions HR can:
	• Create jobs
	• Edit jobs
	• Delete jobs
	• View applicants
	• Shortlist candidates
	• Forward shortlisted candidates to Employer
	• Schedule interviews
	• Manage assigned candidates

22. High-Level Functional Workflow
	1. Candidate uploads resume with minimal details.
	2. The platform immediately extracts the candidate's name and personalizes the interface.
	3. Full resume parsing continues asynchronously in the background.
	4. Candidate data is enriched automatically.
	5. AI evaluates the resume against active job requirements.
	6. The system generates a match percentage and detailed gap analysis.
	7. If additional clarification is required, contextual screening questions are presented.
	8. Candidates browse and apply for jobs (with or without an account).
	9. HR reviews applications, shortlists suitable candidates, and forwards them to the employer.
	10. Qualified candidates schedule interviews through the platform.
	11. Recruiters receive real-time updates throughout the hiring process.
	12. Analytics, referrals, notifications, and feedback operate continuously in the background.

23. Core Product Principles
	• Prioritize user experience with fast and minimal onboarding.
	• Keep the UI responsive by offloading heavy processing to background workflows.
	• Build modular components with loose coupling.
	• Use AI to assist, not replace, recruiter decision-making.
	• Support secure, scalable, and maintainable architecture.
	• Design for incremental evolution from MVP to enterprise scale.
	• Remain technology-agnostic so implementation details can be chosen based on project needs.
